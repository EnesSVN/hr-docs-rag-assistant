# Annual Leave Policy (synthetic sample, not a real document)

## Entitlement
All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their working hours. New employees earn leave from their first day, counted per full month worked.

## Requesting leave
Leave must be requested at least five working days in advance through the HR portal. The line manager answers within two working days. Requests longer than ten consecutive working days also need approval from the department head.

## Carry-over
Unused leave may be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of five days. Carried-over days must be used by the end of March, otherwise they expire.

## Public holidays
Public holidays are not counted as leave days. If a public holiday falls inside a leave period, the leave balance is not reduced for that day.

## Leaving the company
When employment ends, unused leave is paid out with the final salary. If an employee has taken more leave than they earned, the difference is deducted from the final salary.
