# Remote Work Policy (synthetic sample, not a real document)

## Who can work remotely
Employees who have completed their probation period of three months may work remotely. Roles that need physical presence, such as reception and warehouse staff, are not eligible.

## How many days
Eligible employees may work from home up to two days per week. The days are agreed with the team and must be entered in the shared team calendar. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, when everyone must be reachable.

## Approval
The line manager approves the remote work arrangement once. It is reviewed every six months. The manager can withdraw approval with two weeks' notice if the arrangement does not work for the team.

## Equipment and costs
The company provides a laptop. Employees working remotely receive a one-time home office allowance for a desk chair and monitor. Internet and electricity costs are not reimbursed.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is not allowed without written approval from HR, because tax and insurance rules differ between countries. Requests must be made at least thirty days before travel.

## Data security
Company data may only be handled on company devices. Public wifi must be used together with the company VPN.
