# Expense Reimbursement (synthetic sample, not a real document)

## What can be claimed
Employees can claim business travel, accommodation, meals during business trips and client entertainment that was approved in advance. Personal expenses and fines are never reimbursed.

## Limits
The daily meal allowance during business trips is 40 euros. Hotel stays above 150 euros per night need approval from the department head before booking. Train and economy flights are the default; business class needs approval from a director.

## How to submit
Expenses are submitted in the expense tool within thirty days of the expense. Every claim needs a photo of the receipt. Claims without a receipt are only accepted up to 25 euros and need a written explanation.

## Approval and payment
The line manager approves claims within five working days. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary. Claims approved after the 20th are paid with the salary of the month after.

## Company credit card
Employees who travel often may receive a company credit card. The card is only for business expenses and statements must be reconciled every month. Personal use of the card must be paid back within fourteen days.

## Mileage
When employees use their own car for business, mileage is reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre. The route and the purpose of the trip must be written in the claim.
