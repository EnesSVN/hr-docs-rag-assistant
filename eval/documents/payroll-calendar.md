# Payroll Calendar and Payslips (synthetic sample, not a real document)

## Payment dates
Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or a public holiday, payment is made on the previous working day. Payment is made by bank transfer to the account registered in the HR portal.

## Payslips
Payslips are published in the HR portal on the day of payment. They show gross salary, tax, social security contributions and any deductions. Employees can download payslips for the last 36 months.

## Changes to bank details
A change of bank account must be submitted in the HR portal before the 15th of the month to take effect in that month's payment. Changes submitted later apply from the following month.

## Annual bonus
The annual performance bonus is paid together with the March salary. It depends on the result of the yearly performance review and on the company result for the previous year.

## Overtime
Approved overtime is paid with the salary of the following month. Overtime that was not approved in advance by the line manager is not paid.

## Questions about pay
Questions about a payslip should be sent to the payroll team through the HR portal. The team answers within three working days.
